COURSE PURPOSE

From individual media
to integrated systems.

Multimedia combines multiple forms of information—such as text, sound, images, graphics, video and animation—into computer-based experiences. This course follows the path from understanding each media type to storing, processing, synchronizing and delivering integrated multimedia applications.

Use these notes alongside your instructor's lectures and prescribed references. Where the syllabus names a topic without specifying a particular standard or implementation, the notes explain the core concept without assuming a single vendor or version.

Answer-writing framework

  1. Start with a precise definition.
  2. Explain the principle or architecture in logical steps.
  3. Draw a labelled diagram where it adds clarity.
  4. Give a relevant example or application.
  5. Close with advantages, limitations or a comparison.
◷

Revision cycle

  1. Read a unit and make a one-page summary.
  2. Reproduce key diagrams from memory.
  3. Answer short questions without notes.
  4. Practise calculations and algorithms.
  5. Review weak concepts after 24 hours.
